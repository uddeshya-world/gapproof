# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, by reference: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>. The full text there applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to anyone representing the project in public.

In short, we pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected: respect, constructive and specific feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.
Not acceptable: sexualized language or attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions, or ban a contributor, for conduct they judge inappropriate.

To report a problem, open a private report through GitHub (the repository's *Security* tab, then *Report a vulnerability*, <https://github.com/uddeshya-world/gapproof/security/advisories/new>) and start the title with "Conduct report". Reports are handled privately and the reporter's identity is kept confidential. Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
